iPhone Speaker & Microphone FAQs (Mentor, OH)

Why can’t people hear me on my iPhone calls?

It can be a blocked or failed microphone, a case covering the mic, a Bluetooth device still connected, or a software issue. We’ll test it and tell you what’s actually going on.

My iPhone speaker sounds muffled. Can it just be cleaned?

Sometimes, yes. Lint and debris in the speaker grille are common. If cleaning doesn’t fix it, we’ll quote a speaker replacement upfront.

How long does iPhone speaker or microphone repair take?

Many repairs are same-day, often in 30–60 minutes when the part is in stock. Call (440) 525-5705 to check your model.

Will I lose my data?

Speaker and microphone repairs don’t normally touch your data, but we always recommend a current backup before any repair.

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome. Appointments are preferred to minimize wait time — call (440) 525-5705 to schedule.
