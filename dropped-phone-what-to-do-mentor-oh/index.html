Dropped Your Phone? What to Do Next in Mentor, OH

It slipped out of your pocket in a parking lot, fell off the counter, or took a dive down the stairs. Sometimes a dropped phone is fine. Sometimes the damage is obvious, and sometimes it shows up a day later as a dead touch spot, a blurry camera, or a battery that suddenly drains fast. Check These Right Away

1

Look at the screen and back

Check for cracks, especially at the corners. If glass is cracked, put clear tape over it or keep it in a case so shards don’t cut you.

2

Test the touch screen

Open an app and drag an icon slowly across the whole screen. Dead spots or ghost taps mean the display was damaged, even if the glass looks fine.

3

Try the cameras and flashlight

Take a photo with each rear camera and the front camera. Blurry, shaky, or black images can mean a damaged camera or lens cover.

4

Make a call

Check that the earpiece, speaker, and microphone work and that you have signal.

5

Plug it in

Make sure it still charges normally and the cable doesn’t feel loose.

Stop Using It and Get It Checked If

  • The screen or back is bulging or lifting, which can mean a swollen battery (a safety issue)
  • The phone gets unusually hot or you smell something burning
  • The screen has black spots, lines, or spreading color blotches
  • It fell in water, the toilet, or a puddle as well as hitting the ground
  • It keeps restarting or won’t turn on

If the phone still works but you haven’t backed it up lately, do that first. Damage from a drop can get worse, and a backup protects your photos no matter what happens next.

Dropped Phone FAQs (Mentor, OH)

My phone looks fine after the drop. Should I worry?

Probably not, but run through the checks above. Problems like a dead touch spot, a shaky camera, or a loose charging port sometimes only show up when you test them.

Is it safe to keep using a phone with a cracked screen?

For a short time, usually, if you cover the cracks so glass can’t cut you. Cracks tend to spread and can let moisture in, so it’s better to repair it sooner rather than later.

Why did my battery start draining fast after I dropped it?

A hard drop can damage the battery or loosen a connection. If the battery is also swelling or the phone gets hot, stop using it and bring it in.

Should I fix it or replace the phone?

It depends on the phone’s age and what was damaged. We’ll give you an honest quote, and if a repair doesn’t make sense for your phone, we’ll say so.
