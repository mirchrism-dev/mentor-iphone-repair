Repair or Replace Your Phone? An Honest Guide

When a phone breaks, the first question is usually whether it’s worth fixing at all. Sometimes a repair is the clear winner, and sometimes putting the money toward a new phone makes more sense. Repair Usually Makes Sense When

  • The phone was working fine before one thing broke, like the screen, battery, or charging port
  • The repair costs well under what a comparable replacement phone would cost you
  • Your phone still gets software and security updates
  • You like the phone, it has the storage you need, and you’d rather not set up a new one
  • The battery is the main complaint; a new battery can make an older phone feel much better

Replacing May Make More Sense When

  • Several things are failing at once, for example a cracked screen plus water damage plus a weak battery
  • The repair would cost close to what the phone is worth
  • The phone no longer receives security updates
  • It’s too slow or too small on storage for what you use it for, even when it isn’t broken

Either way, your photos and contacts matter. If you’re replacing a phone that still turns on, back it up first. If it won’t turn on, see phone data recovery before you trade it in or recycle it.

If you planned to upgrade in a few months anyway, a cheap fix or a careful wait may be enough. If you want another couple of years, a quality repair plus a battery can get you there.

Repair or Replace FAQs (Mentor, OH)

Is it worth fixing a cracked iPhone screen?

For most phones that otherwise work well and still get updates, yes. A screen replacement typically costs far less than a new phone. We’ll quote your exact model so you can compare.

Is a new battery worth it on an older phone?

Often it is. If the phone dies quickly or shuts off in the cold but otherwise does what you need, a battery replacement can extend its life for much less than a new phone.

Can you tell me if my phone is worth repairing before I pay anything?

Yes. We look at the phone and give you a clear quote first. If we think replacing it makes more sense, we’ll say so.

What about water damage?

Water damage is harder to predict. Some phones come back fully, others don’t. Bring it in quickly, and we’ll be honest about the odds. See our water damage page.
