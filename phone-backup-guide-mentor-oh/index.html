How to Back Up Your Phone

The worst part of a broken phone usually isn’t the repair. It’s finding out the photos were never backed up. A backup takes a few minutes to set up and then mostly runs on its own. Here’s how to do it on iPhone and Android, and how to check it’s actually working. If you get stuck, stop by Mentor iPhone and Computer at 7745 Mentor Ave, Mentor, OH 44060 or give us a call.

iPhone: Two Ways to Back Up

1

iCloud Backup

Go to Settings, tap your name, then iCloud, then iCloud Backup, and turn it on. It runs when the phone is locked, charging, and on Wi-Fi. Tap “Back Up Now” to start one right away.

2

Back up to a computer

On a Mac, connect the iPhone and open Finder. On Windows, use the Apple Devices app or iTunes. Choose to back up all data to the computer. This works even if you don’t want to pay for iCloud storage.

The free 5 GB of iCloud storage often isn’t enough for a full backup. If it says there isn’t enough space, you can buy more storage or use a computer backup instead.

Android: Google and Samsung Backups

1

Google backup

Open Settings, search for “Backup,” and turn on backup with your Google account. It covers apps, contacts, call history, settings, and texts on most phones.

2

Photos

Google Photos backup is a separate setting. Open Google Photos, tap your profile picture, and make sure backup is on.

3

Samsung phones

Samsung Galaxy phones also offer Samsung Cloud and Smart Switch. Smart Switch can save a full backup to a computer.

Check That It Actually Worked

  • Look for the date of your last successful backup in the backup settings
  • Open your photos on another device or the web to confirm they’re there
  • Make sure you know your Apple Account or Google password; a backup is useless if you can’t sign in
  • Back up again before any repair, upgrade, or trade-in

Phone Already Broken?

If the screen is cracked or black but the phone still turns on, a backup may still be possible. If it won’t power on, see phone data recovery. Getting ready for a repair? Our before-repair checklist covers the rest.

Phone Backup FAQs (Mentor, OH)

Does a repair erase my phone?

Most screen, battery, and charging port repairs don’t touch your data, but a backup is always smart before any repair.

How often should I back up?

Turning on automatic iCloud or Google backup is the easiest answer, so it happens on its own while you charge overnight.

Are my texts backed up?

On iPhone, iCloud Backup or Messages in iCloud covers them. On Android, Google backup covers texts on most phones. Check the settings to be sure.
