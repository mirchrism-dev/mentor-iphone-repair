Phone Water Damage FAQs (Mentor, OH)

What should I do if my phone gets wet?

Power it off if it’s still on, avoid charging it, and bring it in as soon as you can. Don’t use a hair dryer or put it in rice—those can make corrosion worse. We’ll inspect it and explain your options.

Can water-damaged phones be fixed?

Often yes, depending on how much liquid reached the board and how quickly you bring it in. We’ll diagnose the damage and give an honest assessment before any paid repair work.

How long does water damage repair take?

Diagnosis is typically quick. Repair timing depends on the damage and parts needed. Many customers get same-day help when the fix is straightforward; more severe liquid damage may take longer.

Do you offer a warranty on water damage repairs?

We back our repair work with a warranty. Liquid damage can be unpredictable, so we’ll explain what is covered for your specific situation before work begins.

Do I need an appointment for water damage help?

Walk-ins are welcome—especially for liquid damage, sooner is better. Appointments are preferred if you want to minimize wait time — call (440) 525-5705.
