Try These Steps First

1

Check the charger and outlet

Plug into a different wall outlet, not a power strip, and look for a light on the charger or the laptop’s charging LED. A worn cable or adapter is one of the most common causes.

2

Do a power reset

Unplug the charger, remove the battery if your laptop has a removable one, and hold the power button for about 15–30 seconds. Then plug back in and try again.

3

Look for signs it’s actually on

Listen for fans, watch for keyboard or Caps Lock lights, and try an external monitor. If any of those work, the screen may be the problem, not the laptop.

4

Give a dead battery time

A fully drained battery may need 15–30 minutes on the charger before the laptop responds. Leave it plugged in before deciding it’s dead.

Common Reasons a Laptop Won’t Power On

Charger or charging port

A failed adapter or a loose charging jack means no power reaches the laptop. Often a quick fix once we confirm which part it is.

Worn-out battery

An old battery can stop the laptop from starting even when plugged in. A laptop battery replacement often solves it.

Screen or backlight failure

The laptop runs but you see nothing. A laptop screen repair fixes that.

Liquid or motherboard damage

Spills, power surges, and age can damage the board. We’ll be honest about whether repair is worth it on your model.

Turns on but won’t load Windows, or stuck on a loading screen? That’s often a software issue. See Windows PC software support. MacBook owners can also check MacBook repair.

Laptop Won’t Turn On FAQs (Mentor, OH)

Are my files gone if my laptop won’t turn on?

Usually not. In many cases the storage drive is fine even when the laptop won’t start. We won’t wipe anything without asking, and we can talk about getting files off if the laptop can’t be saved.

How do I know if it’s the charger or the laptop?

The charger light, the laptop’s charging LED, and whether a known-good charger works are the quickest clues. Bring both the laptop and charger in and we’ll test them.

My laptop’s lights come on but the screen is black. What’s wrong?

It could be the screen, its backlight, the display cable, or a startup problem. Trying an external monitor helps narrow it down, and we can confirm the cause in the shop.

Is it worth repairing an older laptop that won’t turn on?

Sometimes yes, sometimes no. A charger or battery fix is usually worth it, while major board damage on an older laptop may not be. We’ll give you an honest quote and opinion before any work.
