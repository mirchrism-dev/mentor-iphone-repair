The Checklist

1

Back up your phone

On iPhone, use iCloud Backup or connect to a computer. On Android, use your Google backup or Samsung Cloud. Most repairs don’t touch your data, but a backup means nothing can go wrong with your photos no matter what.

2

Know your passcode

We need to test the screen, touch, cameras, Face ID or fingerprint, and calls after the repair. With your passcode we can confirm everything works before you pick it up. If you’d rather not share it, tell us, and we’ll test what we can with you at the counter.

3

Charge it if you can

A little battery lets us check the phone when you drop it off and test it again afterward.

4

Take off the case and screen protector

Not required, but it saves time. Bring the case so you can put it back on at pickup.

5

Note everything that’s wrong

A crack is obvious, but tell us about anything else too, like a weak speaker or a flaky charging port, so we can check it while the phone is open.

What You Don’t Need to Do

  • You don’t need to factory reset or erase your phone for most repairs
  • You don’t need to remove your SIM card
  • You don’t need the original box or receipt

If your phone won’t turn on or the screen is too damaged to back it up, that’s okay. Bring it in as-is and we’ll go from there. If your data is the priority, mention it right away and see our phone data recovery page.

Before-Repair Checklist FAQs (Mentor, OH)

Should I turn off Find My iPhone before a repair?

Usually not for a screen or battery repair. If your repair needs it, we’ll tell you and walk you through it. Keep your Apple ID password handy just in case.

Will my data be erased during a screen or battery repair?

Normally no. Screen, battery, and charging port repairs don’t erase your phone. We still recommend a backup beforehand, because any electronic device can fail unexpectedly.

My phone is too broken to back up. What now?

Bring it in as it is. If the phone still powers on, we can sometimes get the display working enough for you to back it up, or advise you on data recovery options.

Do I have to leave my phone with you?

For many quick repairs you can wait or grab coffee nearby and come back. We’ll give you a time estimate when you check in.

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome, and appointments are preferred so we can have parts ready. Call (440) 525-5705.
