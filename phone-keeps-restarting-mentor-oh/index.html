Phone Keeps Restarting or Boot-Looping?

A phone that reboots on its own — mid-call, while charging, or stuck looping on the logo — is frustrating and easy to make worse with the wrong “fix.” Causes range from a buggy update or full storage to a failing battery, charging port, or (after a drop or liquid) hardware that needs a shop look. Common Causes (iPhone and Android)

Software after an update

A bad OS update or a crashy app can trigger random reboots until you update again, uninstall the offender, or restore.

Storage almost full

When free space is nearly gone, both iOS and Android can become unstable and restart under load.

Battery or charging trouble

A swollen or failing battery, or a flaky charge port/cable, can brown-out the phone and force a reboot.

Drop, bend, or liquid

Impact and moisture can cause intermittent board or button issues that look like random restarts.

Safe Steps to Try First

1

Note when it restarts

While charging only? When you open one app? After a drop? Patterns help a lot — write them down before you come in.

2

Free some storage

Offload large videos and apps you don’t need. Aim for a few GB free, then see if reboots calm down.

3

Update or remove recent apps

Install pending system updates on Wi-Fi. If reboots started after one app install, delete that app and retest.

4

Try another charger and cable

Rule out a cable that drops power. Avoid no-name bricks that sag under load.

Phone Keeps Restarting FAQs (Mentor, OH)

Why does my phone keep restarting by itself?

Common causes are a buggy update or app, nearly full storage, a failing battery or charger path, or hardware damage after a drop or liquid. The pattern of when it reboots is the best clue.

Is a restart loop different from random reboots?

Yes. Random reboots while using the phone often point to battery, heat, or a bad app. A boot loop that never reaches the home screen is more serious and usually needs hands-on diagnosis.

Can I factory reset to fix it?

Sometimes, after a backup. But if the cause is hardware (battery, port, liquid), a reset won’t help and you’ll have wiped the phone for nothing. Try the lighter steps first, or bring it in before erasing.
