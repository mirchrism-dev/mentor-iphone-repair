Try These Steps First

1

Clean and dry the screen

Water, lotion, or grease can confuse the touch sensor. Wipe the screen with a soft, dry cloth, and dry your hands too.

2

Take off the case and screen protector

A cracked, lifting, or thick protector, or a case pressing on the edges, can cause dead spots and phantom touches. Test the phone bare.

3

Force restart

A frozen app or system glitch can make the screen ignore you. A force restart (the button combination depends on your model) often clears it without erasing anything.

4

Unplug the charger

Some phones get ghost touches only while charging, especially with a cheap or damaged cable. If the problem stops when unplugged, try a different charger.

If the phone was dropped or got wet and the screen is now unresponsive, don’t keep charging and testing it for days. Liquid damage can spread, so see our phone water damage page.

What Usually Causes It

A damaged screen

Even a small crack, or a drop with no visible crack, can damage the touch layer. Usually the fix is a new screen: see iPhone screen repair, Samsung screen repair, or Google Pixel repair.

Ghost touch

Phantom taps can come from a damaged display, a low-quality replacement screen from a past repair, liquid, or a bad charger. Narrowing down which one it is matters before replacing anything.

Software or an app

If the screen works fine on the lock screen or after a restart and acts up only in one app, it may be software. Updating the phone and the app often helps.

A swelling battery

A battery that’s starting to bulge can press on the screen from behind. If the screen is lifting or the phone looks puffy, stop using it and bring it in. Our iPhone battery replacement page has more.

Touch Screen Not Working FAQs (Mentor, OH)

Why is my phone screen touching by itself?

That’s called ghost touch. Common causes include a damaged screen, a low-quality replacement screen, moisture, a faulty charger, or a lifting screen protector. Try the phone unplugged and without a protector first. If it keeps happening, we can diagnose it.

Can you fix just the touch part of the screen?

On most modern phones the touch layer is built into the display, so the usual fix is replacing the screen assembly. We’ll confirm the cause and quote it before any work.

Part of my screen works and part doesn’t. What does that mean?

A dead zone in one area usually points to damage in the touch layer, often from a drop or pressure. It can also be caused by a protector or case, so test without them first.

Can I back up my phone if the screen won’t respond?

Often yes. An iPhone may still back up to iCloud if that’s turned on, or to a computer it already trusts. Some Android phones can be reached with a mouse through a USB-C adapter. If you’re stuck, we can talk through options, and our phone data recovery page has more.
