Quick Checks You Can Do at Home

1

Give it a hard surface

Beds, couches, and blankets block the air vents underneath. Use the laptop on a desk or table and see whether the fan calms down.

2

Find out what’s working it hard

Open Task Manager on Windows or Activity Monitor on a Mac and sort by CPU. A stuck program or a browser with dozens of tabs can keep the fan running nonstop.

3

Look at the vents

Shine a light into the side and rear vents. A gray layer of dust or pet hair means air isn’t getting through. Don’t poke anything inside the vents.

4

Use the original charger

A wrong or failing charger can cause odd power and heat behavior. Try the charger that came with the laptop if you have it.

If the bottom case is bulging, the trackpad is lifting, or you smell something hot, stop using it and unplug it. Those can be signs of a swelling battery.

Common Causes of Laptop Overheating

Dust in the fan and heatsink

Over time, dust packs into the fan and the cooling fins, so the laptop can’t move heat out. This is the most common cause in older laptops.

Dried-out thermal paste

The paste between the processor and the heatsink can dry out after a few years, so heat doesn’t transfer as well.

A failing fan

A fan that grinds, clicks, or doesn’t spin at all can’t keep the laptop cool, even with clean vents.

Software keeping it busy

Background programs, unwanted software, or a stuck update can keep the processor working hard all the time.

Laptop Overheating FAQs (Mentor, OH)

How hot is too hot for a laptop?

Warm is normal, especially under load. If it’s too hot to keep on your lap, the fan is loud all the time, or it slows down or shuts off on its own, it’s worth having it checked.

Can overheating damage my laptop?

Laptops protect themselves by slowing down or shutting off, but running hot day after day is hard on the battery and other parts. Fixing the cause early is usually simpler.

Should I buy a cooling pad?

A cooling pad can help a little, but it won’t clear dust inside or fix a failing fan. It’s better to find out why the laptop is running hot.
