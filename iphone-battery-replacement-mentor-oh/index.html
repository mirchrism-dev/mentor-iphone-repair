iPhone Battery Replacement FAQs (Mentor, OH)

How long does iPhone battery replacement take?

Most battery replacements are completed in about 30–60 minutes, depending on model and parts availability.

Do you offer a warranty on battery replacements?

Yes. We back our repairs with a warranty. Ask in-store for the details that apply to your battery replacement.

Common signs include sudden shutdowns, rapid drain, unexpected heat, or a battery health warning. Bring it in and we’ll check it at no charge before any work starts.
