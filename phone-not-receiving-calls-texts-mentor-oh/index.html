Phone Not Getting Calls or Texts?

When people say they called but your phone never rang, or texts show up hours late, it’s rarely a broken phone. A setting, a blocked number, or a carrier issue is usually behind it. Quick Checks

1

Look for Focus or Do Not Disturb

On iPhone, open Control Center and make sure no Focus (like Sleep or Do Not Disturb) is on. On Android, swipe down and check the Do Not Disturb tile. Scheduled modes can turn on by themselves at night.

2

Check blocked numbers and unknown-caller filters

On iPhone, look in the Phone settings for Blocked Contacts and Silence Unknown Callers. On Android, open the Phone app’s settings for blocked numbers and spam filtering. A filter can quietly send real calls to voicemail.

3

Make sure calls aren’t being forwarded

Look for Call Forwarding in your phone settings and turn it off unless you set it up on purpose. Your carrier can also check this for you.

4

Restart and update

Restart the phone, then install any software update. Updates can include carrier settings that fix calling and texting problems.

Tip: on newer iPhones, Phone and Messages settings are under Settings, then Apps.

Only Missing Some Texts?

Switched from iPhone to Android

If texts from iPhone users stopped arriving, your number may still be registered with iMessage. Turn off iMessage on the old iPhone, or use Apple’s online tool to deregister your number.

Group texts missing

Turn on MMS Messaging and Group Messaging in your messages settings, and make sure cellular data is on.

Texts landing in spam

On Android, check the Spam & blocked folder in Messages. On iPhone, check the Unknown Senders list if message filtering is on.

Storage nearly full

A full phone can have trouble receiving pictures and videos. See iPhone storage full.

When It’s Signal or Hardware

If your phone shows No Service or SOS, start with iPhone No Service. If calls connect but you can’t hear the other person, or they can’t hear you, read iPhone speaker and microphone repair. Problems that started after a drop or a spill are worth having us look at in person.

Missed Calls & Texts FAQs (Mentor, OH)

Why do my calls go straight to voicemail?

Usually because Do Not Disturb or a Focus is on, the caller is blocked or filtered as unknown, call forwarding is turned on, or the phone has no signal at that moment.

Why can’t I get texts from iPhone users after switching to Android?

Your number may still be tied to iMessage. Turn iMessage off on the old iPhone or deregister your number with Apple’s online tool, then ask a friend to send a test text.

Is this a carrier problem or a phone problem?

If the settings above look right and another phone with your SIM works fine, it points to your phone. If problems continue with a different phone, call your carrier.
