Common Causes

A failing display

After a drop, pressure in a pocket, or simply years of use, the screen or its connector can start to flicker, flash, or show lines.

Adaptive brightness or Extra dim

Brightness features that constantly adjust can look like flickering, especially indoors under mixed lighting.

A bad cable or case pressure

A cheap charging cable that causes power dips, or a tight case pressing the frame, can make the screen flash briefly.

An app or system glitch

A recent update or a single app can cause odd display behavior that goes away in Safe Mode.

Safe Steps to Try First

1

Turn adaptive brightness off

Open Settings, then Display, and turn off Adaptive brightness (wording varies by Galaxy model). See if the flicker stops.

2

Boot in Safe Mode

Hold power, then touch and hold Power off until Safe Mode appears. If the screen is steady there, an app is likely the cause.

3

Check for a software update

Install any pending system update. Sometimes Samsung ships a fix for display bugs.

4

Remove the case and try another charger

Rule out pressure on the frame and a flaky cable before assuming the screen is bad.

Samsung Screen Flickering FAQs (Mentor, OH)

Why is my Samsung screen flickering?

Common causes are a failing display or connector, adaptive brightness, a bad charger, case pressure, or a software glitch. Persistent flicker that survives Safe Mode usually points to hardware.

Can software cause a Galaxy screen to flash?

Yes. Adaptive brightness, Extra dim, a buggy app, or a bad update can all look like hardware failure. That’s why Safe Mode and brightness settings are worth trying first.

Is flickering a sign I need a new screen?

If it continues after the software checks, or you also have lines, black blotches, or touch problems after a drop, a display repair is often the answer.
