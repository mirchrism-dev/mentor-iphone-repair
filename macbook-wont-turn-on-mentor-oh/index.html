MacBook Won’t Turn On?

When a MacBook won’t start, the first question is whether it’s truly dead or just showing nothing on the screen. A Mac that’s running with a black display, a battery that drained all the way down, and a charger that quietly stopped working can all look the same at first. Work through the checks below. Is It Dead, or Just Dark?

No sound, no light, no warmth

Likely a power problem: the charger, the cable, the battery, or the charging circuit inside the Mac.

Keyboard lights up or you hear a sound, but the screen is black

The Mac may be running fine with a display or backlight problem. Try an external monitor if you have one.

Apple logo appears, then it shuts off or loops

Usually a startup or software issue rather than a power one. macOS Recovery is the next step.

It died after a spill

Unplug it, don’t keep trying to power it on, and bring it in. See laptop water damage repair.

Safe Things to Try First

1

Charge it for 30 minutes

Use the Apple charger and cable if you have them, plugged straight into a wall outlet. A completely drained battery can take a while before the Mac responds. If your charger seems to be the issue, see MacBook won’t charge.

2

Try a different outlet and port

On MacBooks with more than one USB-C port, try another port. On MagSafe models, check the connector for lint or debris.

3

Force a restart

Hold the power button (Touch ID button on newer models) for about 10 seconds, let go, then press it again normally.

4

Unplug everything else

Remove hubs, drives, and docks. A faulty accessory can sometimes stop a Mac from starting.

On Intel MacBooks, resetting the SMC can help with power issues; on Apple silicon (M1 and later) Macs, a full shutdown and a 30-second wait does the same job.

MacBook Won’t Turn On FAQs (Mentor, OH)

My MacBook is black but I think it’s on. What does that mean?

If you hear sounds, feel warmth, or see the keyboard light up, the Mac may be running with a display problem. An external monitor test can help tell the difference, and we can check it in the shop.

Will I lose my files?

Not necessarily. A Mac that won’t power on often still has its data intact. Tell us up front if your files matter so we can plan around them.

Can a bad charger make it seem dead?

Yes. A failed charger or cable is one of the first things we rule out, because it’s the cheapest fix.

Is it worth fixing an older MacBook?

Sometimes yes, sometimes no. We’ll give you an honest answer. Our repair or replace guide covers how to think about it.
