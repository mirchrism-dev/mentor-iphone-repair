Laptop Has No Sound?

When a laptop goes silent, it’s easy to assume the speakers died. More often, the sound is just being sent somewhere else: a Bluetooth speaker you used last week, a monitor, or headphones. Drivers and updates are the next most common cause. Work through the checks below. If the speakers are still quiet or crackling, bring it to Mentor iPhone and Computer at 7745 Mentor Ave, Mentor, OH 44060 and we’ll figure out whether it’s software or hardware.

Narrow It Down First

Headphones work, speakers don’t

The software side is probably fine. Check the output setting, then suspect the speakers or the headphone jack sensor.

No sound anywhere, even with headphones

Usually a settings, driver, or update problem rather than broken speakers.

Crackling, buzzing, or very quiet

Often a worn or damaged speaker, especially after a drop or a spill.

Sound stopped after a Windows or macOS update

Very likely a driver or settings change. Start with the software steps below.

Things to Try

1

Check volume and mute

Make sure the laptop isn’t muted, and check the volume inside the app or website you’re using too.

2

Pick the right output

On Windows, click the speaker icon on the taskbar and choose your laptop’s speakers. On a Mac, open System Settings, then Sound, then Output. Turn off Bluetooth for a minute to rule out a paired speaker or earbuds.

3

Unplug anything in the headphone jack

Plug headphones in and out once. A stuck jack sensor can make the laptop think headphones are still connected.

4

Restart, then update or reinstall the audio driver

On Windows, open Device Manager, expand “Sound, video and game controllers,” and update the audio driver, or uninstall it and restart so Windows reinstalls it. Windows also has a built-in sound troubleshooter.

If sound works in some apps but not others, the problem is that app’s settings, not your speakers.

Laptop No Sound FAQs (Mentor, OH)

Are my laptop speakers blown?

Maybe, but check the output setting and drivers first. If headphones sound fine and the speakers crackle or stay silent, the speakers are more likely the issue.

Can you replace laptop speakers?

It depends on the laptop model and parts availability. Call (440) 525-5705 with the make and model and we’ll let you know.

Do you work on MacBooks too?

Yes. See our MacBook repair page, or bring it in for a look.

Will fixing a driver erase my files?

Reinstalling an audio driver doesn’t touch your documents or photos. It’s still smart to keep a backup.
