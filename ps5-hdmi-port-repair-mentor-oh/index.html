PlayStation Checks to Try at Home

1

Try another cable, input, and TV

Use the HDMI cable that came with the console if you still have it, switch to a different HDMI input, and test on a second TV or monitor if one is handy. Plug straight into the TV, skipping any soundbar or receiver.

2

Start the PS5 in Safe Mode

With the console fully off, press and hold the power button. Let go after the second beep (about seven seconds). Connect the controller with a USB cable and press the PS button. If a Safe Mode menu shows up, the port is passing video and the problem is more likely a settings or software issue.

3

Change the video output in Safe Mode

From the Safe Mode menu, choose the option to change video output and try a lower resolution. A TV that can’t handle the console’s current setting can look exactly like a dead port.

4

Look inside the port

Shine a flashlight into the HDMI port. Bent, flattened, or missing pins, a port that wiggles, or a cable that won’t seat flat are signs of physical damage. Don’t force a cable in; that can push the pins further out of place.

No picture even in Safe Mode on a second TV? That points to hardware. Stop testing and bring it in.

What We Check Before Quoting

The port itself

We inspect the pins and how solidly the port is attached to the board. On the PS5 and PS4 it’s soldered to the main board, so a replacement is board-level work, not a snap-in part.

The video circuit behind it

No signal doesn’t always mean the port. Other parts of the board that handle video can fail too. We test first so you don’t pay for a port that won’t solve it.

A real-screen test

Once repaired, we check picture and sound on a display before you pick it up.

PS5 HDMI Port Repair FAQs (Mentor, OH)

How can I tell if my PS5 HDMI port is broken?

Bent or missing pins, a loose port, or a picture that drops when the cable moves are the clearest signs. If you get no picture in Safe Mode with a known-good cable on a second TV, the port or the video circuit behind it is the likely cause. We can confirm it in the shop.

Do you work on the PS4 too?

Yes. Bring in a PS4, PS4 Slim, PS4 Pro, or any PS5 model and we’ll diagnose it and explain your options before any work is done.

Will I lose my games or saved data?

Replacing the HDMI port doesn’t require wiping the console’s storage. If you have PlayStation Plus, it’s still a good idea to make sure your saves are backed up to the cloud before any repair.

How long does a PS5 HDMI port repair take?

It depends on how much damage we find once it’s open. We’ll give you a realistic timeline along with the quote after we look at it.
