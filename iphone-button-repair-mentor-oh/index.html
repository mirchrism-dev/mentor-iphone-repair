Quick Checks Before You Come In

1

Take the case off

Thick or warped cases are a surprisingly common cause of buttons that seem broken. Try pressing the button with the case removed.

2

Restart the phone

A frozen app can make buttons feel dead. If the side button won’t work, you can restart from Settings › General › Shut Down.

3

Turn on AssistiveTouch as a stopgap

Settings › Accessibility › Touch › AssistiveTouch puts an on-screen button for volume, lock, and restart until the repair is done.

4

Note when it started

Tell us if the problem began after a drop, a spill, or an update. It helps us find the cause faster.

iPhone Button Repair FAQs (Mentor, OH)

Can you fix an iPhone power button that won’t click?

Usually, yes. A stuck or dead side button is often a worn button part or a damaged flex cable inside the phone. We diagnose it first and quote the repair before starting.

Will I lose Touch ID if my Home button is replaced?

On iPhones with a Home button, Touch ID is tied to the original button, so a replacement button typically works for pressing but not for fingerprint unlock. We’ll talk through that before doing anything.

My mute switch flips on its own. Is that fixable?

Often it is. A loose switch or a damaged cable is the usual cause, and debris can play a part too. We’ll check it and tell you what it needs.

How long does an iPhone button repair take?

Many are done the same day, often in 30–60 minutes when parts are in stock. Some models take longer because more of the phone has to be opened; we’ll give you a time estimate up front.
