PS5 Won’t Turn On?

A PS5 that won’t start can mean very different things. No light at all usually points to power: the cord, the outlet, or the console’s internal power supply. A beep followed by a shutoff, or a light that pulses and never settles, can mean the console is stuck in rest mode, overheating, or has a software problem. Try the checks below first. What the Lights Usually Mean

No light, no sound

The console isn’t getting power or the internal power supply has failed. Start with the cord and outlet.

One beep, then nothing

It may be stuck in rest mode or shutting itself off. A full power cycle and Safe Mode often help.

Blinking white or blue light that never settles

Often a software or update problem. Safe Mode lets you restart, rebuild the database, or reinstall the system software.

Turns on, but your TV says no signal

The PS5 may be fine and the HDMI path is the problem. See PS5 HDMI port repair.

Safe Things to Try First

1

Power cycle it

Unplug the power cord from the back of the PS5 for at least a few minutes, then plug it straight into a wall outlet instead of a power strip.

2

Try a different cord

The PS5 uses a standard two-prong figure-8 power cord. If you have a spare from another device, try it.

3

Hold the power button

Hold the power button for about 10 seconds until it beeps twice. That can bring it out of a stuck rest mode.

4

Start in Safe Mode

With the console off, hold the power button until you hear a second beep (about 7 seconds), then connect the controller with a USB cable and press the PS button. Choose Restart first before trying anything that erases data.

Avoid “Reset PS5” in Safe Mode unless you’re okay losing games and saves that aren’t backed up to PlayStation Plus or a USB drive.

When It Might Be Heat or Dust

A PS5 that runs for a while and then shuts off, or that’s loud before it dies, may be overheating. Give it space to breathe and keep it off carpet. See PS5 overheating and loud fan help for more.

PS5 Won’t Turn On FAQs (Mentor, OH)

My PS5 has no light at all. Is it dead?

Not necessarily. Try another outlet and power cord first. If there’s still nothing, the internal power supply or another part may have failed, and we can check it.

Will Safe Mode erase my games?

Restart and Rebuild Database don’t erase your games or saves. “Reset PS5” and “Reinstall System Software” with a full reset do, so back up first if you can.

It turned off during a storm or power outage. What now?

Unplug it, wait a few minutes, and plug it into a different outlet. Surges can damage the power supply, so if it won’t come back, bring it in.
