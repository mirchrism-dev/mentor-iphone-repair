Rule Out the Easy Stuff First

1

Swap the cable and the TV input

Try a different HDMI cable—ideally the one that came with the console—and a different HDMI input on the TV. If you can, test on a second TV or monitor too.

2

Plug straight into the TV

If the Xbox runs through a soundbar, receiver, or capture card, connect it directly to the TV to take those out of the picture.

3

Power-cycle the console

Hold the Xbox button on the console for about 10 seconds until it shuts down, unplug the power cord for a minute, then turn it back on.

4

Look inside the port with a flashlight

Bent or flattened pins, a crooked or loose port, or debris inside are strong signs of physical damage. Don’t force a cable in if it doesn’t seat normally.

Still no picture after all four? Stop wiggling the cable—that can make a damaged port worse—and bring it in.

What an HDMI Port Repair Involves

A board-level job

On Xbox consoles the HDMI port is soldered to the main board, so replacing it means opening the console, removing the old port, and fitting a new one. It isn’t a snap-in part.

Not always the port

No signal can also come from other video circuitry on the board. We test before we quote, so you aren’t paying for a port that won’t fix it.

Tested on a real display

After the repair, we check the picture and sound on a screen before you pick it up.

Xbox HDMI Port Repair FAQs (Mentor, OH)

How do I know if my Xbox HDMI port is broken?

Bent or missing pins, a port that feels loose, or a picture that cuts out when the cable moves are the clearest signs. If a known-good cable and a different TV still show no signal, the port or the console’s video circuitry is likely the cause. We can confirm it in the shop.

Which Xbox models can you look at?

Bring in your Xbox One, One S, One X, Series S, or Series X and we’ll diagnose it and tell you your options before any work is done.

Will I lose my games or saves?

Repairing the HDMI port doesn’t involve wiping the console’s storage. Games and saves synced to your Xbox account are also stored online, so it’s a good idea to make sure cloud saves are on.

How long does an Xbox HDMI port repair take?

It depends on the console and whether any other damage turns up. We’ll give you a realistic timeline with the quote after we look at it.
