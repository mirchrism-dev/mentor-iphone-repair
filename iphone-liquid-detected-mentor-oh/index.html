iPhone Says “Liquid Detected”?

Newer iPhones check the charging port for moisture before they let power flow. If they sense liquid or something conductive in the port, you get a “Liquid Detected” alert and wired charging stops. That’s a safety feature, not necessarily a sign the phone is ruined. Most of the time the port just needs to dry. Why You’re Seeing the Alert

The port really is wet

Rain, a sink splash, a sweaty pocket, or a drink. Even a little moisture in the port can trigger it.

It came from the cable

A damp or damaged cable end can set it off too. Check the cable, not just the phone.

Debris or corrosion in the port

Lint and grime can hold moisture, and old corrosion can trigger the alert even when the phone feels dry.

It keeps coming back for days

If the phone hasn’t been near water and the alert won’t clear, there may be corrosion or damage inside the port.

What to Do Right Now

1

Unplug the cable

Disconnect the cable from the phone and the power adapter. Don’t keep plugging it back in to test it.

2

Tap out the water

Hold the iPhone with the port facing down and gently tap it against your hand to help liquid drain out.

3

Let it air-dry

Leave it in a dry spot with some airflow. Wait at least 30 minutes before trying to charge again, and longer if the alert comes back.

4

Use wireless charging if you need to

If you have a MagSafe or Qi charger, the phone can usually still charge wirelessly while the port dries.

The alert offers an “Emergency Override” option. Only use it in a real emergency. Charging a wet port can damage the port and the phone.

What Not to Do

  • Don’t put the phone in a bag of rice; Apple specifically advises against it, and small grains can end up in the port
  • Don’t blow it out with a hair dryer or heat it up
  • Don’t stick cotton swabs, paper towels, pins, or anything else into the port
  • Don’t use compressed air jammed into the opening

When to Bring It In

If the alert won’t go away after the phone has had time to dry, if charging is still spotty once it clears, or if the phone was actually dropped in water, we can take a look. Liquid Detected Alert FAQs (Mentor, OH)

Is my iPhone water damaged if I see this alert?

Not necessarily. The alert only means moisture or something conductive was sensed in the port. Many phones are fine once the port dries.

How long does it take to clear?

Often 30 minutes to a few hours. If it’s still there after a day of drying in a normal room, have it checked.

The alert pops up but my phone was never wet. Why?

Lint, corrosion, or a damaged cable can cause it. Try a different Apple or certified cable first, then call us.
