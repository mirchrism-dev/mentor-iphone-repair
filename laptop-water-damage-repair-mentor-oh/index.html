Laptop Water Damage Repair in Mentor, OH

Right After the Spill

1

Shut it down and unplug it

Hold the power button until it turns off, then unplug the charger. Don’t wait to save your work. Every minute it stays powered on while wet raises the risk of a short.

2

Flip it over

Open the laptop into a tent shape, keys facing down, so liquid can drain out instead of soaking into the board.

3

Disconnect everything

Unplug USB drives, mice, and docks. If your model has a battery you can pop out without tools, remove it.

4

Wipe what you can reach

Blot the outside and the keyboard with a dry, lint-free cloth. Don’t shake it hard and don’t press liquid further into the keys.

5

Don’t try to power it back on to test it

Even if it seems fine, turning it on before it’s been checked is the most common way a small spill becomes a dead board. Bring it in instead.

Skip the rice and the hair dryer. Rice doesn’t pull liquid out of a sealed laptop, and heat can push moisture deeper or warp parts. Sugary drinks like soda and coffee with sugar leave sticky residue that keeps corroding even after they dry, so those spills are worth bringing in quickly.

What We Do With a Wet Laptop

Open it up and clean it

We take the laptop apart, look for where the liquid reached, and clean residue and early corrosion from the board and connectors.

Check what’s damaged

Common casualties are the keyboard, trackpad, battery, charging circuit, and the logic board itself. We test parts rather than guessing.

Protect your files

If the laptop can’t be saved, the drive often can. We’ll talk with you about getting your photos and documents off before anything else.

Laptop Water Damage FAQs (Mentor, OH)

Is my laptop ruined if it got wet?

Not necessarily. Many laptops survive a spill if they’re shut off quickly and cleaned before corrosion sets in. The sooner we can look at it, the better the chances.

It still works after the spill. Should I still bring it in?

It’s worth having it checked, especially after coffee, soda, or anything sugary. Residue can keep corroding parts and cause failures days or weeks later.

Can you save my files from a water-damaged laptop?

Often, yes. Storage drives frequently survive even when the board doesn’t. We’ll check the drive and tell you what we can recover.

Do you work on MacBooks with liquid damage?

Yes. We work on MacBooks and Windows laptops. Many newer MacBooks have parts soldered to the board, so we’ll explain what that means for repair options on your model.

Do I need an appointment?
