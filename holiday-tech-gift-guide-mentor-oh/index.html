Before You Buy

1

Match the phone to the carrier

A carrier-locked phone may not work on the person’s plan. An unlocked phone, or one from their own carrier, avoids that surprise. If you’re not sure, ask the carrier.

2

Pick enough storage

Photos and videos fill phones fast. If the person never deletes anything, pay for the next size up rather than living with a full phone.

3

Know the return and warranty terms

Keep the gift receipt. For refurbished or used devices, buy from a seller that offers a return window and warranty. Our used iPhone checklist and used laptop checklist show what to look for.

4

Add protection

A case and a screen protector cost far less than a screen repair. See how to protect your phone from damage.

Gift-by-Gift Tips

Phone for a kid

Plan screen-time limits and parental controls before it’s unwrapped. See kid’s first phone setup.

Phone for a parent or grandparent

Bigger text, louder ringer, and fewer apps help. See phone settings for seniors.

Laptop or computer

Plan on time for updates the first day. See new computer setup.

Game console

Check that the TV has a free HDMI port and the house Wi-Fi reaches the room. An extra controller saves arguments.

Setup Day

  • Charge it before you wrap it, so it’s ready to use when it’s opened.
  • Write down the Apple ID or Google account password somewhere safe, and add a recovery phone number or email.
  • Moving from an old phone? See transfer data to a new phone, then wipe the old one before passing it on.
  • Install updates right away. The first update is often a big one.

Tech Gift FAQs (Mentor, OH)

Should I buy an unlocked phone as a gift?

Often, yes, because it gives the person a choice of carriers. Just make sure the model supports their carrier’s network.

Is a refurbished phone or laptop a good gift?

It can be a good value from a reputable seller with a warranty and return window. Check battery health and condition when it arrives.

What if the gift arrives damaged or stops working?

Contact the seller or manufacturer first while it’s under warranty. Opening it up elsewhere can affect that warranty.

Can you fix it if it breaks after the holidays?
