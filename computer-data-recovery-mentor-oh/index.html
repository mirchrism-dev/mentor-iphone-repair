Common Situations We See

Computer won’t boot

Windows or macOS won’t load, but the drive inside may be fine. We can often remove it or connect to it directly and copy your files to a new drive.

Laptop with a broken screen or board

If the computer itself is dead from a drop or a spill, the storage may still be readable once it’s out of the machine (on many models it can be removed).

External or USB drive not showing up

A bad cable, enclosure, or connector is sometimes the real problem. We test those before assuming the drive is gone.

Accidentally deleted files

Recently deleted files can sometimes be recovered if the drive hasn’t been used much since. Stop using it and bring it in.

Stop Before You Make It Worse

  • If a hard drive is clicking, grinding, or beeping, turn it off and don’t keep retrying
  • Don’t run free recovery apps on a drive that’s making noise or failing; it can damage it further
  • Don’t reinstall Windows or reset the computer if you still need files on it
  • After deleting files by mistake, stop saving new things to that drive

Honest limits: drives with physical internal damage (clicking or grinding hard drives, for example) need a clean-room recovery lab, which we are not. If that’s your situation, we’ll tell you so up front rather than charge you for attempts that won’t work. Some MacBooks and laptops have storage soldered to the board or encrypted, which also limits what any shop can recover.

Computer Data Recovery FAQs (Mentor, OH)

Can you recover files from a laptop that won’t turn on?

Often yes, if the drive inside is healthy. A dead laptop usually means a problem with the board, power, or screen, not the storage. On many models we can read the drive directly. Some laptops have storage soldered to the board, which makes it harder, and we’ll tell you if that’s the case.

My hard drive is clicking. Can you fix it?

A clicking or grinding hard drive usually has internal mechanical damage. That needs a clean-room data recovery lab, and continuing to power it on can make things worse. Turn it off, and we can explain your options.

Do you guarantee you’ll get my files back?

No. Nobody honest can guarantee data recovery. We’ll tell you what we find and what’s realistic before you pay for recovery work.

Where will my recovered files go?

We can copy them to a new external drive or USB stick, or straight onto a new computer if you’re replacing the old one. Bring your own drive if you have one.
