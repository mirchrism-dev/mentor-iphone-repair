MacBook Display Problems We Fix

Cracked or shattered glass

Impact cracks, lid-closed-on-something cracks, and corner damage on MacBook Air and MacBook Pro models.

Dark blotches and bleeding

Black or purple “ink” spots that spread across the image after pressure or a drop, a sign the LCD layer itself is damaged.

Flickering, lines, or color bands

Vertical lines, flashing, or a picture that cuts out at certain lid angles, often a worn display cable rather than the panel.

No image, Mac still on

You hear the startup chime or see the keyboard light, but the screen stays black. We test with an external display to narrow it down.

Before You Bring Your MacBook In

1

Plug into an external monitor

If the picture shows up on a TV or monitor, your Mac and data are probably fine and the problem is likely the screen or its cable.

2

Back up while you can

Use an external display or Time Machine to save your files now, especially if the image is getting worse.

3

Close the lid gently, and don’t press on cracks

Pushing on a cracked panel can spread the damage or push glass into the backlight. Check the keyboard for anything that could press on the screen.

4

Write down your model

Apple menu › About This Mac shows the model and year. Calling with that info lets us check part availability before you drive over.

MacBook Screen Repair FAQs (Mentor, OH)

Can you fix just the glass on a MacBook screen?

On most modern MacBooks the glass and the display panel are bonded together, so a crack usually means replacing the display assembly rather than just the glass. We’ll confirm what your model needs and quote it before starting.

My MacBook screen is black but the Mac seems to be running. Is it the screen?

Not always. A black screen can come from the panel, the backlight, the display cable, or the logic board. Connecting an external monitor is a quick first test, and we’ll diagnose the rest before quoting.

Why does my MacBook screen flicker only when I move the lid?

That pattern often points to a worn display cable that flexes each time the lid opens and closes. It’s worth getting checked early, since it tends to get worse over time.

Will I lose my files during a MacBook screen repair?

A display repair doesn’t normally touch your storage, so your files should stay as they are. We still recommend a backup before any repair, and we won’t erase anything without asking.
