MacBook Won’t Charge? Here’s What to Check

A MacBook that won’t charge usually comes down to one of four things: the charger, the cable, the port, or the battery. Sometimes it’s just a setting. Before you buy a new adapter or assume the worst, try the checks below. If it’s still not charging, bring it to Mentor iPhone and Computer at 7745 Mentor Ave, Mentor, OH 44060 and we’ll find out which part is actually at fault.

Try These First

1

Swap the obvious parts

Try a different wall outlet, then a different cable if you have one. USB-C cables wear out, and a cheap cable may not carry enough power to charge a MacBook.

2

Check the adapter’s wattage

A low-watt phone charger can keep a MacBook alive but not actually charge it, and macOS may say “Not Charging.” Use the adapter that came with the Mac or one with equal or higher wattage.

3

Look in the port

Lint and grit build up in USB-C and MagSafe ports. Shine a light in. If you see debris, don’t dig with metal; we can clean it safely.

4

Check the battery settings

Open System Settings, then Battery. Optimized Battery Charging can pause at 80% on purpose. Battery Health may also say Service Recommended, which points to the battery itself.

5

Restart the Mac

On Apple silicon Macs a full shut down and restart resets the charging controller. On older Intel models, Apple’s SMC reset steps can help.

What We Check in the Shop

Charger and cable

We test your adapter and cable against known-good ones so you don’t replace the wrong part.

Charging port

Bent pins, debris, or a loose port. Many MacBooks have more than one USB-C port, so we test them all.

Battery

An old or swollen battery can refuse to charge. A swollen battery can push up the trackpad, so stop using it and bring it in.

Liquid or board issues

If there’s been a spill or the problem is on the logic board, we’ll explain what we found, what can be done, and the cost before anything else.

MacBook Charging FAQs (Mentor, OH)

Why does my MacBook say “Not Charging” while plugged in?

Usually the adapter is too weak, Optimized Battery Charging is holding the battery at 80%, or the battery needs service. Check the battery settings and try a full-power adapter first.

My MacBook only charges at certain angles. What’s wrong?

That usually points to a worn cable or a loose or dirty port. Try another cable first. If it still only works when you hold it a certain way, bring it in so we can inspect the port.

Is a swollen MacBook battery dangerous?

It should be taken seriously. Stop using the laptop, don’t charge it, and don’t press on the case. Bring it in so we can check it and quote a replacement.

Can you fix it the same day?

It depends on the cause and whether parts are in stock. Cleaning a port or confirming a bad charger can be quick. We’ll give you a time estimate when we see it.
