Quick Checks Before You Bring It In

1

Update the controller and console

Install any pending system and controller updates. On Switch, PlayStation, and Xbox, controller firmware updates are handled through the console’s settings.

2

Recalibrate the sticks

The Switch has a built-in stick calibration tool under Controllers and Sensors in System Settings. Calibration won’t fix a worn stick, but it does rule out a setting problem.

3

Test it on a second game or device

If the drift shows up everywhere, it’s the controller. If it’s only in one game, check that game’s dead-zone or sensitivity settings.

4

Skip the canned air for now

Blasting air or spraying cleaner under the stick sometimes helps for a day and can push debris further in. If drift keeps coming back, the stick module is usually worn.

Drift that started right after a drop or a spill is a different problem, so mention that when you call.

Why Controllers Drift

Worn stick sensors

Each thumbstick reads its position through small sensors that wear with use. As they wear, the controller reports movement when the stick is centered.

Dirt and debris

Dust, crumbs, and skin oils work their way under the stick cap over time, especially on controllers that get passed around a lot.

Drops and liquid

A hard drop can knock the stick module out of alignment, and spills can leave residue on the board under it.

Controller Drift FAQs (Mentor, OH)

Can controller drift be fixed, or do I need a new controller?

Often it can be fixed by cleaning or replacing the worn stick module. Whether that’s worth it depends on the controller and its condition, so we check it first and tell you honestly if a replacement makes more sense.

Which controllers can you look at?

Bring in Nintendo Switch Joy-Cons, PlayStation 5 DualSense controllers, and Xbox controllers. If you have something else, call and ask.

Will fixing one Joy-Con fix the other?

No. Each Joy-Con has its own stick, so if both are drifting, bring both in and we’ll check each one.

How long does a drift repair take?

It depends on the controller and what we find. We’ll give you a realistic timeline with the quote after we look at it.
