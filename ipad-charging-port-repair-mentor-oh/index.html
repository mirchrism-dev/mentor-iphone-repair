Signs Your iPad’s Port Needs Attention

  • The cable won’t click in, feels loose, or falls out
  • It only charges when the cable is held at an angle
  • Charging stops and starts on its own
  • A USB-C keyboard, drive, or display stopped connecting through the port
  • You can see bent contacts, corrosion, or packed lint inside

Seeing “Not Charging” in the status bar? First try the adapter that came with your iPad—a small phone charger often can’t supply enough power while the screen is on. If the right adapter doesn’t help, the port or battery likely needs a look.

iPad Charging Port Repair FAQs (Mentor, OH)

Do you repair both Lightning and USB-C iPad ports?

Yes. Older iPads use Lightning and newer ones use USB-C; we service both. Call with your model so we can confirm the part.

Why does my iPad say “Not Charging” when it’s plugged in?

Often the adapter is too weak—try the one that came with your iPad. If it still says “Not Charging,” the port, cable, or battery may be at fault, and we can test it.

Is it the charging port or the battery?

A loose fit or angle-only charging points to the port. If it charges fine but drains fast, it’s more likely the battery. We’ll diagnose it before recommending a repair.

How long does iPad charging port repair take?

Many are done the same day when the part is in stock. Timing depends on the model, so call ahead for a realistic window.
