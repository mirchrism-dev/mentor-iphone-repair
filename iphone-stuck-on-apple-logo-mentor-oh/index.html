iPhone Stuck on the Apple Logo or Restarting Over and Over?

An iPhone that shows the Apple logo and never finishes starting up is scary, mostly because of what’s on it: photos, messages, and contacts. The good news is that a stuck logo has several possible causes, and some can be fixed without erasing anything. Here’s what usually causes it, what you can safely try, and when to bring it to Mentor iPhone and Computer at 7745 Mentor Ave, Mentor, OH 44060 before you try anything that wipes the phone.

Common Causes

An update that didn’t finish

If the phone lost power or ran out of storage during an iOS update, it can get stuck partway through startup.

Storage completely full

An iPhone with no free space at all can struggle to start, especially right after an update.

A worn or failing battery

A battery that can’t deliver steady power may let the phone reach the logo and then shut off, over and over.

Damage from a drop or liquid

A hit or water exposure can damage a part the phone checks at startup, which can cause a restart loop.

Safe Steps to Try First

1

Plug it in for 30 minutes

Use a cable and charger you trust. A nearly dead battery can cause a loop that stops once it has some charge.

2

Force restart

On iPhone 8 and newer, press and release volume up, press and release volume down, then hold the side button until the logo appears again. Older models use different buttons.

3

Try an update, not a restore

With a computer, Apple’s recovery mode offers Update or Restore. Update tries to reinstall iOS while keeping your data. Restore erases the phone.

4

Stop before choosing Restore

If you don’t have a recent backup, don’t choose Restore. Call us first so we can talk through options.

iPhone Stuck on Apple Logo FAQs (Mentor, OH)

Why is my iPhone stuck on the Apple logo?

Common reasons are an update that didn’t finish, storage that’s completely full, a worn battery, or damage from a drop or liquid. The fix depends on which one it is.

Will I lose my photos if my iPhone is stuck on the logo?

Not necessarily. A force restart or an Update in recovery mode keeps your data. Choosing Restore erases the phone, so only do that if you have a backup.

Can a bad battery cause a boot loop?

Yes. A battery that can’t hold steady power can let the phone start, then shut off and start again. A battery replacement often fixes that kind of loop.

What does the computer-and-cable screen mean?

That’s recovery mode. The phone is asking to be connected to a computer to update or restore iOS. Pick Update first if you want to keep your data.
