iPhone Says “No Service” or “No SIM”? What to Do in Mentor, OH

Your iPhone shows “No Service,” “SOS,” or “No SIM,” and calls and texts stop working. Sometimes the cause is your carrier, sometimes it’s a setting, and sometimes it’s the phone itself, especially after a drop or water exposure. This guide walks through the checks in the order that usually solves it fastest. Try These First, in This Order

1

Check whether it’s just you

Ask someone on the same carrier nearby if their phone works, or check your carrier’s outage page from Wi-Fi. If there’s an outage, there’s nothing to fix on the phone.

2

Toggle Airplane Mode, then restart

Turn Airplane Mode on for about 15 seconds, then off. If that doesn’t help, restart the iPhone completely.

3

Install any carrier settings update

With Wi-Fi on, go to Settings > General > About. If a carrier settings update is available, a prompt will appear. Also make sure iOS is up to date.

4

Check the SIM or eSIM

For a physical SIM, eject the tray, make sure the card isn’t bent or dirty, and reseat it. For an eSIM, go to Settings > Cellular and make sure the line is turned on. If the line is missing, your carrier may need to reissue it.

5

Reset network settings

Settings > General > Transfer or Reset iPhone > Reset > Reset Network Settings. This clears saved Wi-Fi passwords but not your photos or apps.

Account, billing, or eSIM activation problems have to go through your carrier. A repair shop can’t fix those, so call them first if your account was recently changed.

Signs It’s the Phone, Not the Carrier

  • The problem started right after a drop, a cracked back, or water exposure
  • Your SIM works in another phone, but no SIM works in yours
  • The signal comes and goes when you press on or bend the phone slightly
  • “No Service” returns after every restart and network reset
  • Wi-Fi also stopped working, or the phone gets unusually warm near the top

These point to hardware such as a damaged SIM reader, antenna connection, or board-level fault. We’ll diagnose it before quoting, and if a repair isn’t worth it on your model, we’ll tell you.

iPhone No Service FAQs (Mentor, OH)

What does SOS mean in the status bar?

It means your iPhone can’t connect to your own carrier but can still place emergency calls through another network. It usually points to a coverage, account, or SIM issue, and the steps above are the place to start.

Will resetting network settings erase my data?

No. It removes saved Wi-Fi networks and passwords, VPN settings, and Bluetooth pairings, but leaves your photos, messages, and apps alone.

My phone says No SIM even with a new SIM card. What now?

If a new SIM from your carrier still isn’t detected, and that SIM works in another phone, the SIM reader or a related part of the phone may be damaged. We can diagnose that in the shop.

Can you fix an iPhone that lost signal after water damage?

Sometimes. Water can damage antenna connections and board components. We’ll open it, check the extent of the damage, and tell you honestly what’s repairable before you pay for a repair.
