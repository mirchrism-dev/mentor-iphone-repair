Getting a New Phone? How to Move Everything Over

A new phone should feel like an upgrade, not a weekend of hunting for lost photos and two-factor codes. Most of the move is built into iPhone and Android now. The trouble spots are usually old backups, authenticator apps, and switching between iPhone and Android. Before You Start

  • Charge both phones and connect to the same Wi-Fi
  • Know your Apple Account or Google account password, not just the phone passcode
  • Make a fresh backup of the old phone (iCloud, Google, or a computer)
  • Open any authenticator app and check how it moves to a new phone; some codes don’t transfer automatically
  • Keep the old phone until you’ve confirmed photos, texts, and banking apps work on the new one

Pick the Right Method

iPhone to iPhone

Quick Start: hold the new iPhone near the old one and follow the prompts. You can transfer directly or restore from an iCloud backup.

Android to Android

Most Androids offer to copy from your old phone during setup with a cable or wirelessly. Samsung phones also offer Smart Switch.

Android to iPhone

Apple’s Move to iOS app copies contacts, messages, photos, and more during iPhone setup. Some apps need to be reinstalled and signed into again.

iPhone to Android

Many Androids can copy from an iPhone during setup. Turn off iMessage on the old iPhone first so texts from iPhone friends still reach you.

After the Move, Double-Check

1

Photos and videos

Scroll back to your oldest photos. If you use iCloud or Google Photos, let them finish syncing on Wi-Fi.

2

Texts and contacts

Send a test text to an iPhone friend and an Android friend, and look up a few contacts.

3

Two-factor and banking apps

Sign in to email, banking, and anything that sends you codes before you erase the old phone.

4

Then wipe the old phone

Sign out of your accounts and erase it before you sell, trade, or hand it down.

Moving to a New Phone FAQs (Mentor, OH)

Will my text messages transfer to a new phone?

Between two iPhones or two Androids, usually yes. Between iPhone and Android, Apple’s and Google’s setup tools move most messages, but always check before erasing the old phone.

My iCloud is full. Can I still transfer?

Yes. iPhone Quick Start can move data directly from the old iPhone to the new one, and you can also back up to a computer.

Why aren’t I getting texts after switching to Android?

Your number may still be registered with iMessage. Turn off iMessage on the old iPhone, or deregister your number on Apple’s website.

My old phone’s screen is broken. Can I still get my data off?

Often, yes. Sometimes a screen repair is the easiest path, and sometimes the data can be reached another way. Bring it in and we’ll tell you the options and cost first.
