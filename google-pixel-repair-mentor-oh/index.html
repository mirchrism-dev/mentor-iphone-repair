Pixel-Specific Things Worth Knowing

A lifting screen can mean a swollen battery

If your Pixel’s display is separating from the frame or the phone looks puffy, stop charging it and bring it in. That’s usually the battery, not the screen.

Fingerprint unlock after a screen repair

Pixels read your fingerprint through the display. After a screen replacement we test fingerprint unlock with you and explain up front anything about your model that needs extra setup.

Back up before you come in

Turn on Google One / Android backup and make sure your photos are syncing to Google Photos. It only takes a minute and protects you no matter what the repair is.
