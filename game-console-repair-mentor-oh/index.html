Common Console Problems by System

PlayStation

Xbox

Signs It’s Time to Stop Playing and Bring It In

  • A burning or electrical smell, or the console is too hot to touch — unplug it right away
  • The HDMI port is bent, loose, or the picture cuts in and out when the cable moves
  • It powers on for a second and then shuts off, or won’t turn on at all
  • A drink was spilled on it or a controller
  • The Switch screen or back is bulging — that can be a swelling battery

When you come in, bring the console plus its power cable. If the problem involves a controller or dock, bring those too so we can test the whole setup. Please remove any discs or game cards you want to keep with you.

Game Console Repair FAQs (Mentor, OH)

My console turns on but there’s no picture on the TV. Is it the HDMI port?

Sometimes. First try a different HDMI cable, TV input, and TV. If it still shows nothing—or the picture flickers when the cable moves—the console’s HDMI port or video circuit may be damaged. We’ll diagnose it and quote the repair.

Can you fix Joy-Con or controller stick drift?

Drift is one of the most common console complaints. Bring the controller in and we’ll check it and give you your options, including whether a repair or a replacement makes more sense.

My Nintendo Switch won’t charge. What should I try?

Try the original charger, plug it in for at least 30 minutes, then hold the power button for about 15 seconds. If it still won’t charge or dock, the USB-C port or battery may need attention—bring it in for a diagnosis.

How long does a console repair take?

It depends on the console and the part involved. We’ll give you a realistic timeline along with the quote after we look at it.
