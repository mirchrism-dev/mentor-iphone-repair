Samsung Galaxy Back Glass Repair in Mentor, OH

Many Samsung Galaxy phones, including most Galaxy S and Note models, have a glass back that can crack just as easily as the screen. What We Fix

Cracked or shattered back glass

We remove the broken back panel, clean off the old adhesive, and fit a new back with fresh adhesive.

Cracked camera lens cover

If the small glass cover over the rear cameras is cracked, photos can look hazy or have flare. We can often replace that along with the back.

Damage that came with the drop

A hard drop can also crack the screen, bend the frame, or loosen parts. We check those while the phone is open and tell you what we find.

Not sure if your model has a glass back? Many Galaxy A-series phones use a plastic back instead. Call with your model and we’ll tell you what it needs.

Why Not Leave It Cracked?

  • Loose glass shards can cut your fingers or snag pockets
  • Cracks let in dust and moisture that the phone’s seals would normally keep out
  • Cracks usually spread, and pieces can fall out over time
  • A cracked back can lower what you get if you trade in or sell the phone

Samsung Back Glass FAQs (Mentor, OH)

Is a cracked back just cosmetic?

Not entirely. It can cut your hands, it lets dust and moisture in, and the cracks tend to spread. It’s also worth checking whether the drop damaged anything else.

Will my phone still be water resistant after the repair?

We seal the new back with fresh adhesive, but no repair shop can guarantee the original factory water resistance rating after a phone has been opened. We’d still recommend keeping it away from water.

Will wireless charging still work?

Wireless charging should work normally after a back glass replacement. We test it before you pick up the phone.

My back is lifting but not cracked. Is that the same problem?

Not always. A back that’s lifting or bulging can mean the battery is swelling, which is a safety issue. Stop charging it and bring it in so we can check the battery.
