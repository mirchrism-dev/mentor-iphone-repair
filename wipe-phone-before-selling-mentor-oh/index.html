How to Wipe Your Old Phone Before You Sell or Trade It In

Getting a new phone is the easy part. The old one still has your photos, messages, saved passwords, and accounts on it, and handing it off without erasing it properly can cause problems for you and for the next owner. Before You Erase Anything

1

Make sure your data is on the new phone

Check that your photos, contacts, and messages made it over, or that you have a full backup. See transfer data to a new phone.

2

Save two-factor codes

If you use an authenticator app, move it to the new phone first, or you could get locked out of accounts.

3

Unpair accessories

Unpair an Apple Watch or other watch from the old phone so it can be set up with the new one.

4

Remove your SIM card

If your phone uses a physical SIM, take it out. If it uses an eSIM, make sure your service is working on the new phone first.

How to Erase It

iPhone

Open Settings, tap General, then Transfer or Reset iPhone, then Erase All Content and Settings. You’ll be asked for your Apple ID password, which also turns off Find My and Activation Lock.

Android

Remove your Google account first in Settings under Accounts, then use Factory data reset in Settings. The exact menu names vary by brand, so search Settings for “reset” if you don’t see it.

Samsung

Samsung phones may also have a Samsung account. Remove it along with your Google account before resetting.

Why Signing Out Matters

  • An iPhone that still has Activation Lock on can’t be set up by the next owner, which can hold up a sale or trade-in
  • Androids have a similar lock that asks for the previous Google account after a reset if you didn’t remove it first
  • Signing out of your accounts helps keep your information off the device

Wiping an Old Phone FAQs (Mentor, OH)

Is a factory reset enough to wipe my phone?

On modern iPhones and Androids, a factory reset erases your data. The important extra step is signing out of your Apple ID or Google account first so the next owner isn’t locked out.

What happens if I forget to turn off Find My?

The next owner will see an Activation Lock screen asking for your Apple ID. You can remove the device from your account at iCloud.com, but it’s easier to sign out before you erase.

Should I remove my SIM card before selling?

Yes, take out a physical SIM. Your SIM is tied to your phone number and service, not the phone itself.

My old phone’s screen is broken. Can I still wipe it?

Sometimes it can be erased remotely through Find My or Google’s Find My Device, but a working screen makes it much easier. A screen repair may also raise the trade-in value. Ask us for a quote.
