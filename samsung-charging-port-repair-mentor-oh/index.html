Samsung Charging Port Repair FAQs (Mentor, OH)

Why won’t my Samsung Galaxy charge?

Common causes include lint in the port, a worn or damaged USB-C port, a bad cable or charger, moisture, or a battery problem. We’ll test it and tell you which one it is.

How long does Samsung charging port repair take?

Many Galaxy charging port repairs are done the same day, often in about 30–60 minutes depending on model and parts availability.

Can a dirty charging port be cleaned instead of replaced?

Often, yes. Packed lint is a common cause of loose or failed charging. If a careful cleaning fixes it, we’ll tell you rather than replace parts you don’t need.

My Galaxy keeps saying moisture detected. Can you help?

Yes. If the warning keeps showing after the phone has fully dried, the port may be corroded or damaged. Bring it in and we’ll check it.

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome. Appointments are preferred if you want to minimize wait time — call (440) 525-5705 to schedule.
