Phone Charger & Cable Guide

A lot of “my phone won’t charge” visits start with the cable, not the phone. Cheap cables wear out fast, charge slowly, and can heat up or damage a charging port over time. What to Look For

Brand-name or certified

Use the charger from your phone’s maker or a known brand. For Lightning cables, look for Apple’s “Made for iPhone” (MFi) label. For USB-C, USB-IF certified cables from reputable brands are a safe bet.

Enough power

Most recent iPhones fast-charge with a 20W or higher USB-C adapter. Many Samsung Galaxy phones support Super Fast Charging with a compatible 25W adapter. A higher-watt adapter is fine; the phone only takes what it needs.

A snug fit

The plug should click in and stay put. A loose fit usually means lint in the port or a worn cable end.

Wireless that matches your phone

Qi chargers work with most recent phones. iPhone 12 and later also work with MagSafe chargers.

Warning Signs to Stop Using a Cable

  • Fraying, exposed wire, or a bent connector
  • The plug or cable gets noticeably hot
  • Charging only works if you hold it at an angle
  • Your iPhone says the accessory “may not be supported”
  • Burn marks or a burning smell; unplug it and stop using it right away

Good Charging Habits

1

Charge on a hard surface

Not under a pillow or blanket, where heat builds up.

2

Keep the port clean

Pocket lint packs into charging ports over time. Don’t dig at it with metal objects like pins or paper clips.

3

Don’t pull by the cord

Grip the plug to unplug. Yanking the cable is how most connectors break.

4

Skip the gas-station cables

Unbranded bargain cables are the ones we see fail most often.

Phone Charger & Cable FAQs (Mentor, OH)

Can a cheap charger damage my phone?

It can. Poorly made chargers and cables may overheat, charge unevenly, or wear out the port faster. Stick with your phone’s brand or certified accessories.

Is it okay to charge overnight?

Modern iPhones and Android phones manage charging to protect the battery, and many have an optimized or adaptive charging setting. Charge on a hard surface with a good cable.

Why does my phone charge so slowly?

Common causes are a low-watt adapter, a worn cable, lint in the port, or a battery that’s aging. Our battery draining fast page covers battery wear.

Can you tell if it’s my cable or my phone?
