Apple Watch Problems We Help With

Cracked or shattered screen

Watch faces are small, but a crack can still spread, let in sweat and moisture, and stop touch from working. We replace damaged displays when parts for your model are available.

Battery drains by mid-day

If your watch used to last all day and now needs Low Power Mode by lunch, or Settings shows reduced battery health, a battery replacement can bring back its runtime.

Screen lifting or bulging

A display that’s popping up from the case is often a swelling battery underneath. Stop charging it and bring it in—this is one to have checked sooner rather than later.

Won’t charge or won’t power on

Before you buy a new watch, we’ll test it with a known-good charger and check whether it’s the charger, the battery, or something deeper.

Before You Drop Off Your Watch

1

Make sure it’s backed up through your iPhone

Apple Watch data syncs to the paired iPhone. Keep your phone updated and nearby so your health and activity data is safe before any repair.

2

Know your Apple ID password

If the watch ever needs to be re-paired after a repair, Activation Lock will ask for it. Having it handy saves you a second trip.

3

Bring the charger if you suspect charging trouble

If it won’t charge, bring your magnetic charging cable too. A worn cable or adapter is a common culprit, and it’s free to rule out.

Apple Watch Repair FAQs (Mentor, OH)

Can a cracked Apple Watch screen be fixed, or do I need a new watch?

In many cases the display can be replaced. It depends on your model and part availability, so call (440) 525-5705 with your series and case size (for example 41mm or 45mm) and we’ll give you an honest quote.

Is it safe to keep wearing a watch with a cracked screen?

Small cracks can have sharp edges and can let in sweat and water, so avoid swimming or showering with it. If the glass is lifting or the case looks swollen, stop charging it and bring it in.

How do I check my Apple Watch battery health?

On the watch, open Settings, then Battery, then Battery Health. If maximum capacity is noticeably low or it says service is recommended, a battery replacement is worth considering.

Will I lose my data during the repair?

Your watch data is synced to your paired iPhone, so keeping that phone backed up protects your health and activity history. We’ll walk you through anything you need to do before drop-off.

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome, but appointments are preferred for watch repairs so we can confirm parts for your model first.
