iPad Screen Repair FAQs (Mentor, OH)

How long does iPad screen repair take?

Many iPad screen replacements finish the same day, often in about 30–60 minutes when the display for your model is in stock. Call ahead to confirm parts.

Do you repair iPad Air, iPad Pro, and iPad mini screens?

Yes. We work on a wide range of iPad models including Air, Pro, mini, and standard iPads. Bring yours in so we can confirm the part and quote before any work starts.

Is iPad screen repair warranty-backed?

Yes. We back our repairs with a warranty. Ask in-store for the details that apply to your iPad screen repair.

Will I lose my data when the screen is replaced?

A screen replacement does not wipe your iPad. We still recommend backing up to iCloud or a computer first, and unlocking the device so we can test touch and display after the repair.

Do I need an appointment for iPad screen repair?

Walk-ins are welcome. Appointments are preferred if you want to minimize wait time — call (440) 525-5705 to schedule.
