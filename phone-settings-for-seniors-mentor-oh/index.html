Make Your Phone Easier to Use

Phones ship with tiny text, quiet ringers, and a lot of settings most people never touch. A few changes can make an iPhone or Android much easier to see, hear, and use, whether it’s your own phone or one you’re setting up for a parent or grandparent. These are built into the phone and free. Exact menu names vary by model and software version. Easier to See

Bigger text

iPhone: Settings, then Display & Brightness, then Text Size. Android: Settings, then Display, then Font size.

Bold text and display zoom

Bold text makes letters easier to read. Display zoom (iPhone) or display size (Android) makes buttons and icons bigger too.

Brighter screen

Turn up brightness and consider turning off auto-brightness if the screen keeps dimming too much.

Magnifier

iPhone has a built-in Magnifier app that uses the camera to enlarge menus and labels. Many Android phones have a similar magnification feature.

Easier to Hear

1

Turn up the ringer

Check the ringer volume separately from media volume, and make sure the phone isn’t on silent.

2

Pick a louder ringtone

Some ringtones are much easier to hear than others. Vibration on top helps too.

3

Flash for alerts

Both iPhone and Android can flash the camera light when a call or message comes in.

4

Hearing aid pairing

Many hearing aids connect to phones over Bluetooth. iPhone has a Hearing Devices setting under Accessibility, and Android has hearing aid support under Accessibility on many models.

If calls still sound muffled at full volume, the speaker or microphone may need attention. See speaker and microphone repair.

Safer and Less Stressful

  • Turn on Silence Unknown Callers (iPhone) or call screening / spam filtering (Android) to cut down on scam calls
  • Set up Medical ID (iPhone) or emergency information (Android) so first responders can see contacts and conditions from the lock screen
  • Add favorite contacts with photos so calling family is one tap
  • Learn the warning signs of scams. See signs your phone is hacked and scam pop-up help
  • Make sure backups are on so a lost or broken phone doesn’t mean lost photos

Easier Phone Settings FAQs (Mentor, OH)

How do I make the text bigger on my phone?

On iPhone, go to Settings, then Display & Brightness, then Text Size. On most Android phones, go to Settings, then Display, then Font size. Bold text helps too.

How can I stop so many scam calls?

Turn on Silence Unknown Callers on iPhone, or call screening or spam protection on Android. Calls from numbers not in your contacts go to voicemail instead of ringing.

My phone is turned all the way up and I still can’t hear calls.

Check that the earpiece isn’t blocked by a case or screen protector, and try speakerphone. If it’s still quiet, the speaker may be worn or clogged, and that can be repaired.

What is Medical ID?

It’s a feature that lets emergency responders see important health information and emergency contacts without unlocking the phone. Android has a similar emergency information setting.
