If You Just Spilled on Your Keyboard

1

Shut it down and unplug it

Turn the laptop off right away and disconnect the charger. Power running through wet parts is what does the most damage.

2

Tip it upside down

Open it into a tent shape, keys facing down, so liquid drains out instead of soaking in deeper. Wipe what you can reach.

3

Skip the hair dryer and rice

Heat can push liquid further in, and rice doesn’t pull moisture out of a laptop. Leave it off and bring it in.

4

Tell us what was spilled

Water, coffee, soda, and juice behave differently. Sugary drinks leave sticky residue, so it helps us to know.

Keyboard Problems We Fix

Dead keys or rows

Several keys stop at once, which often points to a keyboard or connector issue rather than the keys themselves.

Sticky or crunchy keys

Residue from spills or crumbs under the keys. Sometimes a cleaning is enough; sometimes the keyboard needs replacing.

Missing or broken keycaps

Keys that popped off or have broken clips. We’ll check whether the cap can go back on or needs a replacement part.

Repeating or wrong characters

Keys that type on their own or type the wrong letter, from hardware damage or a language or settings mix-up.

Laptop Keyboard Repair FAQs (Mentor, OH)

Can you fix my laptop keyboard after a spill?

Often, yes, especially if the laptop was turned off quickly. We check the keyboard and the parts underneath it, since spills can reach the motherboard, and tell you honestly what it needs.

Do you replace single keys?

When the key and its clip can be reattached or a matching keycap is available, yes. If the damage is under the key, the keyboard itself may need replacing, and we’ll explain the difference in cost.

Why do some laptop keyboard repairs cost more?

On many thin laptops and MacBooks, the keyboard is attached to the top case, so the whole assembly is replaced. We’ll check your model and quote it before any work.

Will my files be safe?

A keyboard repair doesn’t touch your files, but we always suggest a backup before any computer repair. If the laptop won’t boot, ask us about data options.
