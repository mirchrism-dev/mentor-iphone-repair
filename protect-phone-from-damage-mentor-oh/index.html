Protection That Actually Helps

A case with a raised lip

A case whose edges sit slightly above the screen and camera keeps the glass off the ground when the phone lands face down. Corners matter most.

A tempered glass screen protector

It can take a scratch or crack that would otherwise land on your real screen, and it’s much cheaper to replace.

Back glass coverage

Many iPhones and Samsungs have glass backs. A full case protects the back and camera lenses too. See iPhone back glass repair.

A grip or a secure pocket

Most drops we see happen getting out of the car, at the checkout, or from a back pocket when sitting down.

Everyday Habits That Prevent Repairs

1

Keep the charging port clean

Pocket lint packs into the port and causes loose, slow, or failed charging. Don’t dig with metal; a shop can clean it safely. See phone won’t charge.

2

Use decent cables and chargers

Very cheap chargers can heat up and charge unevenly. Replace cables that are frayed near the ends.

3

Avoid heat while charging

Don’t charge under a pillow or leave the phone on a hot dashboard. Heat wears batteries faster. See iPhone overheating.

4

Respect water resistance limits

Water resistance wears down with age and drops. Keep phones away from pools, hot tubs, and the bathroom counter. If it gets wet, see phone water damage.

Cold weather drains batteries and can make phones shut off early. See our cold weather phone tips.

Phone Protection FAQs (Mentor, OH)

Do screen protectors really help?

A good tempered glass protector can take scratches and some impacts that would otherwise reach your real screen. It won’t stop every crack, especially from corner drops, so pair it with a case that has raised edges.

What kind of case is best?

One that covers the corners and has a lip above the screen and camera. It doesn’t have to be bulky, but very thin shells don’t do much in a drop.

Why does my charging port stop working?

Lint and debris packed into the port is one of the most common causes we see. A careful cleaning sometimes fixes it; if the port is worn, it can be replaced.

Is it bad to charge my phone overnight?

Modern phones manage overnight charging well. Heat is the bigger enemy, so keep it on a hard surface, not under a pillow, and use a decent charger.
