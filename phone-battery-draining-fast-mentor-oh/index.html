If your phone used to last all day and now needs a charger by early afternoon, the cause is usually one of two things: something in software is using more power than it should, or the battery itself has worn out. Check These First

1

Look at battery health

On iPhone, go to Settings, then Battery, then Battery Health (or Battery Health & Charging). Many Android phones show battery information under Settings, then Battery or Device care, though not every model reports health.

2

See which apps are using the most

The battery screen on both iPhone and Android lists usage by app. One app taking a big share, especially in the background, is often the whole problem.

3

Update and restart

Install pending system and app updates, then restart. It’s normal for a phone to run warmer and drain faster for a day or so after a big update while it re-indexes.

4

Trim the background drains

Lower screen brightness, turn off background refresh for apps that don’t need it, and check location access. Weak cell signal also drains a battery quickly, so notice whether it’s worse in certain places.

If the battery is visibly swollen, the screen is lifting, or the phone gets hot while sitting idle, stop charging it and bring it in rather than troubleshooting.

Signs It’s the Battery, Not the Settings

Sudden shutdowns

The phone turns off at 20 or 30 percent, or shuts off in the cold, even though the meter said it had charge left.

Fast drop after full charge

It falls from 100 to the 80s within minutes of unplugging, then drains steadily even with light use.

Low health or a service message

Battery health well below where it started, or a “Service” or “Important battery message” warning on iPhone.

Swelling or heat

A bulging back or a screen pushing up at the edge is a worn, swelling battery and should be looked at soon.

Battery Drain FAQs (Mentor, OH)

How do I know if I need a new battery?

Low battery health, sudden shutdowns with charge showing, or fast drain right after a full charge usually mean the battery is worn. If the battery tests healthy, the drain is more likely an app or setting, and we’ll tell you that.

Does a new battery fix all battery drain?

No. A new battery fixes drain caused by a worn battery. If an app or setting is using the power, a new battery will drain fast too, which is why we check first.

Is it normal for the battery to drain after an update?

For a day or two, yes. Phones often do background work after a major update. If heavy drain lasts longer than that, check the app usage list or bring it in.

How long does a battery replacement take?

Many are same-day, often 30 to 60 minutes, depending on the phone model and whether the part is in stock. Call ahead and we’ll tell you.
