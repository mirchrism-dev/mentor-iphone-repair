iPhone Not Showing Up on Your Computer?

You plug in your iPhone to copy photos or make a backup, and the computer acts like nothing happened. Usually it’s one of three things: the iPhone hasn’t been told to trust the computer, the cable only charges, or the computer is missing the right app. Here are the checks we’d run, from Mentor iPhone and Computer at 7745 Mentor Ave, Mentor, OH 44060.

Things to Try

1

Unlock the iPhone and tap Trust

Unlock your iPhone before you plug it in. A “Trust This Computer?” message should appear. Tap Trust and enter your passcode. If you tapped “Don’t Trust” by mistake, unplug and plug back in, or reset it under Settings, General, Transfer or Reset iPhone, Reset, Reset Location & Privacy.

2

Swap the cable and port

Some cheap cables only carry power, not data. Use an Apple cable or a good-quality one, and try a different USB port directly on the computer instead of a hub. See our charger and cable guide.

3

Use the right app

On a Mac, the iPhone shows up in the Finder sidebar, and photos come in through the Photos app. On Windows, install Apple Devices (or iTunes on older Windows) from Microsoft Store, and use the Photos app or File Explorer to import pictures.

4

Update and restart both

Restart the iPhone and the computer, and install any pending iOS, macOS, or Windows updates. Old software is a common reason the two won’t talk.

Only want your photos on a computer? iCloud Photos or Google Photos can sync them without a cable at all.

Signs It’s the Phone, Not the Computer

  • The iPhone won’t charge from the same cable either, or only charges at an angle
  • Lint is packed into the charging port, or the port looks damaged
  • It doesn’t show up on any computer you try, with several cables
  • The phone recently got wet; see phone water damage repair

iPhone Not Showing Up on Computer FAQs (Mentor, OH)

Why doesn’t the Trust This Computer message appear?

Make sure the iPhone is unlocked when you connect it, and try another cable. A charge-only cable or a dirty port can stop the prompt from showing.

Do I need iTunes on Windows?

On current Windows versions, Apple’s Apple Devices app from Microsoft Store handles syncing and backups. Older Windows setups use iTunes.

My iPhone charges but the computer doesn’t see it. Why?

That usually points to a charge-only cable, a USB hub, the trust setting, or missing software, not a broken phone.

Can you get photos off my iPhone if the port is bad?
