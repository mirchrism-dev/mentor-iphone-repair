PS5 Overheating or Fan Running Loud?

A PS5 that roars during games, shuts itself off, or pops up a temperature warning is telling you heat isn’t getting out. Most of the time the cause is dust, poor airflow around the console, or a fan that’s starting to wear. Signs Your PS5 Is Running Too Hot

  • The fan gets loud within a few minutes of starting a game, even on menus
  • The console turns off by itself, or you see a warning that the PS5 is too hot
  • Games stutter or freeze after you’ve been playing a while
  • The air coming out of the back feels weak, or the console is hot to the touch on top

Things to Try at Home First

1

Give it room to breathe

Keep several inches of open space around the sides and back. Closed TV cabinets, carpet, and tight shelves trap hot air.

2

Stand it or lay it the right way

Use the stand that came with it, whether it’s upright or on its side, so the vents aren’t blocked.

3

Clean the outside vents

With the console off and unplugged, use a soft brush or short bursts of canned air on the intake and exhaust vents. Don’t spin the fan with compressed air.

4

Empty the dust catchers

The PS5 has dust catcher holes you can reach by removing the side covers. Sony shows how in its manual. A vacuum on low around those openings helps.

5

Update the system software

Sony has released updates that change fan behavior. Make sure the console is up to date.

When It Needs a Bench Look

Still loud after cleaning

If the outside is clean and it’s still loud or hot, dust may be packed deeper inside, or the fan may be wearing out.

Grinding, rattling, or clicking

Those sounds point to the fan itself rather than normal airflow noise.

Shutting off during games

Repeated heat shutdowns are worth checking soon, before heat stresses other parts.

After a drop or a spill

A bump or liquid can damage the fan or block airflow in ways you can’t see from outside.

PS5 Overheating and Fan Noise FAQs (Mentor, OH)

Why is my PS5 so loud all of a sudden?

Usually dust has built up or the console has less room to breathe than it used to. Some games also push the system harder. If cleaning the vents doesn’t quiet it down, the fan may need a look.

Is it bad if my PS5 shuts off from overheating?

The shutdown protects the console, but it shouldn’t happen regularly. Fix the airflow first. If it keeps happening, bring it in before heat causes other problems.

Can I clean the inside of my PS5 myself?

You can reach the dust catchers by removing the side covers, as Sony describes. Opening it further takes more care, and it’s easy to damage the fan or ribbon cables.

Should I use canned air on the fan?

Use short bursts on the vents only, and don’t let the fan spin from the air. Spinning a fan that way can wear it out faster.
