Is It the Phone or Just the Screen?

It vibrates, rings, or makes sounds

The phone is probably on and the display is the problem. See Samsung screen repair.

Nothing at all, even on the charger

Could be a fully drained battery, a bad cable, or a dirty or damaged charging port. See Samsung charging port repair.

Stuck on the Samsung logo

Usually a software problem or a failed update, sometimes storage or hardware. Don’t factory reset yet if your photos aren’t backed up.

Turns on only when plugged in

A worn battery is the usual cause. See Samsung battery replacement.

Safe Things to Try First

1

Charge it for 30 minutes

Use a wall adapter and a cable that works on another phone. Look for a charging icon or a warm phone.

2

Force restart

Hold the side (power) key and volume down together for about 10 seconds, until the phone restarts or the Samsung logo appears.

3

Check the port

Shine a light into the USB-C port. Packed lint can stop it from charging. Don’t dig with metal; we can clean it carefully.

4

Call the phone

If it rings or vibrates when someone calls it, the phone works and the screen likely needs repair.

Got wet? Don’t charge it. See water damage help first.

Samsung Won’t Turn On FAQs (Mentor, OH)

My Samsung died and won’t charge. Is it the battery?

It might be, but a bad cable, adapter, or blocked charging port causes the same symptom. We check those first so you don’t pay for a part you don’t need.

Will a force restart erase anything?

No. A force restart just reboots the phone. A factory reset is what erases data, so avoid that until your photos are safe.

My Galaxy is stuck on the Samsung logo. What should I do?

Try a force restart and let it sit on the charger. If it keeps looping, bring it in, especially if you need the data on it.
