Signs Your iPad Needs a New Battery

  • Battery drains quickly even with light use
  • iPad shuts off suddenly or restarts on its own
  • It only stays on while plugged in
  • Charging takes far longer than it used to

iPad Battery Replacement FAQs

How do I know it’s the battery and not the charger?

Fast drain, sudden shutdowns, and only running while plugged in usually point to the battery. If it’s actually the port or cable, we’ll tell you during the check.

How long does an iPad battery replacement take?

Many are done the same day, often in about 30–60 minutes when the battery for your model is in stock. Call ahead to confirm.

Will I lose my apps or photos?

A battery replacement doesn’t normally affect your data, but we always recommend backing up to iCloud or a computer first.

My iPad screen is lifting. Is that safe?

A lifting screen can mean a swollen battery. Stop using and charging it, and bring it in so we can take a look.
