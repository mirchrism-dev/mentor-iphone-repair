Phone Data Recovery FAQs (Mentor, OH)

Can you recover photos and contacts from my phone?

Often we can recover photos, contacts, and other files when the storage is still readable and the device (or a backup) is accessible. Results depend on the damage, encryption, and whether data was overwritten. We’ll give an honest assessment—never a fake guarantee.

Do you guarantee 100% data recovery?

No. We won’t promise what we can’t deliver. Some phones are locked, severely damaged, or wiped beyond recovery. We’ll tell you what’s realistic for your situation before you pay for recovery work.

How long does phone data recovery take?

Initial assessment is usually quick. Recovery time varies—simple transfers can be same-day (often within about 30–60 minutes when the phone powers on), while more complex cases may take longer. We’ll set expectations up front.

What should I bring for data recovery?

Bring the phone, any passcodes or biometrics needed to unlock it, and a USB drive or cloud account if you want files copied off. If the phone won’t turn on, bring it anyway—we’ll still evaluate options.

Do I need an appointment for phone data recovery?

Walk-ins are welcome. Appointments are preferred if you want to minimize wait time — call (440) 525-5705 to schedule.
