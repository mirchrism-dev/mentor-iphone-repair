Still on Windows 10? What End of Support Means for You

Microsoft ended regular support for Windows 10 on October 14, 2025. Your computer didn’t stop working that day, but it no longer gets the routine security updates that patch new holes, so it gets riskier to use online over time. Your Main Options

Upgrade to Windows 11

If your PC meets Microsoft’s requirements, upgrading is usually free and keeps your files and most programs. Older PCs often fail the check because of the processor or a security chip setting.

Extended Security Updates

Microsoft offers a paid or limited Extended Security Updates program for Windows 10 for a period after end of support. It buys time, not a long-term fix. Check Microsoft’s current terms for your situation.

Replace the computer

If your PC can’t run Windows 11 and it’s already slow, a newer computer may be the better spend. The key is moving your files and settings over safely.

How to Check Where You Stand

1

See which Windows you have

Press the Windows key, type winver, and press Enter. The window shows your version.

2

Run the compatibility check

Open Settings, Update & Security, Windows Update. Many PCs show whether they can upgrade to Windows 11 right there. Microsoft’s PC Health Check app also gives an answer.

3

Back up before anything else

Copy your documents, photos, and browser bookmarks to an external drive or cloud storage before upgrading or switching computers.

Be careful: scammers use “your Windows is expired” pop-ups and calls to sell fake support. Microsoft won’t call you about this. If you got a warning with a phone number, read our scam pop-up guide.

Windows 10 End of Support FAQs (Mentor, OH)

Will my Windows 10 computer stop working?

No. It will keep turning on and running your programs. It just stops getting regular security fixes, so using it online becomes riskier as time goes on.

Is the Windows 11 upgrade free?

For PCs that meet Microsoft’s requirements and are running a genuine copy of Windows 10, Microsoft has offered the upgrade at no cost. We can check whether your PC qualifies.

My PC says it can’t run Windows 11. Can that be fixed?

Sometimes it’s just a setting, like a security chip that’s turned off. Other times the processor is too old. We’ll tell you which and what your realistic options are.

Will upgrading delete my files?

A standard upgrade is designed to keep your files and apps, but things can go wrong, so always back up first. We can do the backup for you.
