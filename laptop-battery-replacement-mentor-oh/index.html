Laptop Battery Replacement FAQs (Mentor, OH)

How do I know if my laptop battery needs replacing?

Common signs are short runtime, sudden shutdowns, a laptop that only works plugged in, or a battery warning from the system. We can check battery health for you.

My laptop battery is swollen. Is that dangerous?

A swollen battery should be taken seriously. Stop using the laptop on your lap or bed, keep it unplugged, and bring it in so we can look at it.

How long does a laptop battery replacement take?

It depends on the model and whether the battery is in stock. We’ll give you a realistic estimate when we quote.

Is it worth replacing the battery in an older laptop?

Often a new battery gives an older laptop more useful life, but not always. We’ll give you an honest price and opinion so you can decide.
