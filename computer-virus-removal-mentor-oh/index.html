Signs Your Computer May Be Infected

Browser hijacked

Your homepage or search engine keeps switching to one you didn’t choose, or new tabs open to ad sites.

Mystery programs

Apps, extensions, or “optimizers” you don’t remember installing, sometimes demanding payment to “fix” problems.

Suddenly slow or busy

The fan runs hard and the computer crawls even when you’re not doing much.

Friends got strange emails

Contacts receive messages or links from your email or social accounts that you didn’t send.

Computer Virus Removal FAQs (Mentor, OH)

Can you remove a virus without deleting my files?

In most cases, yes. Our goal is to clean the computer and leave your documents and photos alone. If a reinstall ever becomes the better option, we’ll back up your files first and talk it through with you before doing it.

I already have antivirus. How did I get infected?

Antivirus doesn’t catch everything, especially software you agreed to install by accident, like a fake update or a free download with extras bundled in. That’s one of the most common ways we see unwanted programs get on a computer.

Do you work on Macs too?

Mac malware is less common but does happen, mostly as adware and browser hijackers. Bring it in and we’ll take a look.

How long does virus removal take?

It depends on how much is on the computer and how badly it’s infected. We’ll give you a realistic estimate when you drop it off.
