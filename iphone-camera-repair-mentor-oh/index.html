iPhone Camera Repair FAQs (Mentor, OH)

How long does iPhone camera repair take?

Many camera repairs—including cracked camera glass and module replacements—are completed the same day, often in about 30–60 minutes when parts are on hand.

Why is my iPhone camera blurry or out of focus?

Blurry photos can come from cracked or scratched camera glass, dirt under the lens cover, a failing focus motor, or a damaged camera module. We’ll inspect the lens and camera assembly and tell you what’s causing it before any work starts.

Can you fix a cracked rear camera glass on an iPhone?

Yes. Cracked camera glass is a common repair. We can replace the glass or the full camera module when needed, with honest upfront pricing so you know the cost first.

Do you repair both front and rear iPhone cameras?

Yes. We handle front (selfie) and rear camera issues—including no focus, black screen in Camera app, and physical damage—depending on your model and parts availability.

Do I need an appointment for iPhone camera repair?
