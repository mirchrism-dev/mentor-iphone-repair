iPhone Screen Repair FAQs (Mentor, OH)

How long does iPhone screen repair take?

Most screen repairs are completed in about 30–60 minutes, depending on model and parts availability.

Do you offer a warranty on screen repairs?

Yes. We back our repairs with a warranty. Ask in-store for the details that apply to your repair.
