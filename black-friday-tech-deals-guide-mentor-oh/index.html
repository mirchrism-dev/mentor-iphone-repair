Black Friday and Cyber Monday bring real discounts on phones, laptops, tablets, and earbuds, along with fake shopping sites, knockoff accessories, and “deals” on models that are years old. Spot a Fake Deal Site

1

Type the store’s address yourself

Don’t buy from a link in a text, email, or social media ad. Go to the retailer’s site directly or use its official app. See our scam texts and calls guide.

2

Be wary of prices that are too low

A brand-new iPhone or game console at half price from a site you’ve never heard of is almost always a scam.

3

Pay with a credit card

Credit cards usually make it easier to dispute a charge. Avoid sellers who want gift cards, wire transfers, or payment apps.

4

Check the return policy

A real seller has a clear return window and contact details. If you can’t find them, shop elsewhere.

Check What You’re Actually Getting

Model year and specs

Compare the exact model number, not just the name. Some doorbuster laptops have little memory or storage; look for at least 8GB of RAM and an SSD for everyday use.

Refurbished grades

“Refurbished,” “renewed,” and “open box” mean different things at different stores. Look for a stated battery condition, a warranty, and a return window. Our used iPhone and used laptop checklists help.

Carrier locks

Discounted phones are often tied to a carrier or require a new line. Read the fine print before you buy.

Knockoff chargers and earbuds

Very cheap “brand-name” chargers, cables, and earbuds on marketplace sites are often counterfeit. Fake chargers can damage phones. See our charger and cable guide.

After It Arrives

  • Open and test it right away, while you’re still inside the return window.
  • On an iPhone, check Settings, General, About for the model, and Settings, Battery, Battery Health on refurbished units.
  • Register AirPods or other earbuds with your Apple ID or the maker’s app. Fakes often fail this step.
  • Put a case and screen protector on before the first drop. See how to protect your phone. Buying gifts? Our holiday tech gift guide covers setup day.

Tech Deal FAQs (Mentor, OH)

Are Black Friday tech deals actually good?

Many are, especially from well-known retailers. The risk is fake sites, counterfeit accessories, and older or low-spec models marketed as a bargain.

Is a refurbished phone a good buy?

It can be, if it comes from a reputable seller with a warranty, a return window, and a stated battery condition. Check it for problems as soon as it arrives.

How do I know if my AirPods are fake?

Genuine AirPods pair with an iPhone through Apple’s setup screen and show a serial number you can check on Apple’s coverage site. Very low prices from marketplace sellers are a warning sign.
