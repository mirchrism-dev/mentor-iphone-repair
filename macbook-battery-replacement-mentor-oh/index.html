MacBook Battery Replacement FAQs (Mentor, OH)

How long does MacBook battery replacement take?

Many MacBook battery replacements are completed the same day when the battery for your model is in stock. Timing varies by Air vs Pro and generation — call ahead and we’ll give you a realistic window.

Do you replace batteries on MacBook Air and MacBook Pro?

Yes. We service a wide range of MacBook Air and MacBook Pro models. Bring yours in so we can confirm the part and quote before any work starts.

Is MacBook battery replacement warranty-backed?

Yes. We back our repairs with a warranty. Ask in-store for the details that apply to your battery replacement.

My trackpad feels raised — could that be the battery?

A swollen battery can push up the trackpad or bottom case. Stop using and charging the laptop if you see swelling, and bring it in so we can check it safely.
