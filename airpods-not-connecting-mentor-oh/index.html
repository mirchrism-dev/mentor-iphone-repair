AirPods Not Connecting, Pairing, or Staying Connected?

AirPods that won’t show up, connect to only one ear, or drop every few minutes are usually a pairing, charge, or phone Bluetooth problem — not always “dead” buds. This guide covers the checks that fix most cases. Try These First

1

Charge the case and both buds

Put both AirPods in the case, close the lid for 30 seconds, then open it. A dead bud or case is the most common “won’t connect” cause.

2

Forget the device and re-pair

On iPhone: Settings → Bluetooth → tap the i next to your AirPods → Forget This Device. Then open the case next to the phone and re-pair.

3

Reset the AirPods

With the AirPods in the case, open the lid, press and hold the back button until the status light flashes amber then white (timing varies by model). Pair again.

4

Test with another device

If they connect to a second phone or computer but not yours, the issue is likely on your phone’s Bluetooth side.

What Usually Causes It

One bud out of charge or dirty

Earwax on the sensors or a bud that isn’t seating in the case can stop charging and pairing.

Stale pairing record

After a phone restore, iOS update, or switching phones, old Bluetooth records get confused. Forgetting and re-pairing clears that.

Phone Bluetooth trouble

If every headset fails on the same phone, the phone — not the AirPods — needs attention. See iPhone Wi-Fi and Bluetooth issues.

Firmware or case issues

A case that won’t charge the buds, or buds stuck on old firmware, can cause repeated dropouts.

AirPods Not Connecting FAQs (Mentor, OH)

Why won’t my AirPods connect to my iPhone?

Usually the case or a bud needs charge, the phone still has a stale pairing record, or the AirPods need a reset. Less often, the phone’s Bluetooth is the problem.

Only one AirPod connects. What now?

Charge both in the case, clean the charging contacts gently, reset the AirPods, and re-pair. If one bud still never connects on any device, that bud or the case may be failing.

Do AirPods work with Android?

Yes, as standard Bluetooth headphones. Features like automatic switching and some status pop-ups are iPhone-only. Pairing still uses your Android Bluetooth menu.

Can a phone repair fix AirPods connection problems?

If every wireless headset fails on the same phone, a phone diagnosis is the right next step. If only one pair of AirPods fails everywhere, the buds or case are the likely issue. Call (440) 525-5705 and we can help sort out which side it is.
