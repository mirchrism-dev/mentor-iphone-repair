Laptop Screen Repair FAQs (Mentor, OH)

Can you replace a cracked laptop screen?

Yes. We repair screens on many Windows laptops. Call (440) 525-5705 with your make and model, or stop in, and we’ll confirm what we can do.

How long does laptop screen repair take?

It depends on the model and whether the part is in stock. We’ll give you a realistic estimate when we quote the repair.

My laptop screen is black but the computer turns on. Is it the screen?

Not always. It can be the panel, the display cable, or a graphics or software issue. We’ll diagnose it before recommending a repair.

Is it worth fixing the screen on an older laptop?

Sometimes yes, sometimes no. We’ll give you an honest price and our opinion so you can decide whether to repair or replace.

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome. Appointments are preferred if you want to minimize wait time — call (440) 525-5705 to schedule.
